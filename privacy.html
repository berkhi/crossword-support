Crossword: Word Game: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Crossword: Word Game does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app works offline and makes no network requests.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • Your progress, statistics and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Crash and usage data that you choose to share with developers through iOS settings is collected by Apple and reaches the developer only in anonymous, aggregated form (App Store Connect).

If this policy changes, for example when a future version adds ads, the new policy will be posted here before that version is released.

Yürürlük tarihi: 10 Ekim 2026
